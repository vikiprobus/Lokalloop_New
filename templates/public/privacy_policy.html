LOKALLOOP PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 1 October 2026 • Last Updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who We Are & Scope

2. Personal Data We Collect & Process

In accordance with the principle of data minimization under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, we collect only data reasonably necessary to deliver, secure, and operate the Services:

A. Buyer Account & Profile Data

  • Registration Data: First name, last name, mobile phone number, email address, preferred city, and optional gender.
  • Authentication: Cryptographically hashed passwords or secure authentication tokens. Passwords are never stored in plaintext and cannot be read by platform personnel.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, browser client, session tokens, and security error logs.

B. Foreground Geolocation Data

With your explicit device permission, LokalLoop accesses your approximate or precise foreground location while using the app or website solely to calculate distances and showcase nearby shops and active discount deals. We do not track location in the background when the app is closed. You can disable location permissions in your browser or device settings at any time.

C. Offers, Counter QR Scans & Reward Redemptions

When you redeem a counter deal, we record the offer identifier, shop identifier, transaction timestamp, redemption approval status, and loyalty points ledger entries. The merchant at the counter only sees the verification token and discount details required to validate your purchase; merchants never receive your email address, phone number, or broader account profile.

D. Merchant & Storefront Compliance Data

Sellers submit store registration information directly through our digital Merchant Setup Wizard, including business trade name, physical address, GPS pin, category, authorized signatory name, contact email, mobile phone, and statutory compliance numbers (such as Shop Act license, GSTIN, PAN, and applicable FSSAI/FDA licenses). These compliance documents are audited by authorized verification personnel before merchant privileges are unlocked.

3. Why We Use Personal Data (Purpose Limitation)

  • To create, authenticate, and administer secure buyer and seller user accounts;
  • To calculate proximity and display relevant local store deals and merchant profiles;
  • To process counter QR code validation requests and credit loyalty reward points;
  • To verify merchant identities, prevent fraudulent claims, and stop double-spending of coupons;
  • To send essential transactional notifications, receipts, and security alerts (non-promotional);
  • To comply with statutory legal obligations, taxation laws, and lawful government requests.

4. Consent & Withdrawal of Consent

Processing is carried out on the basis of your freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent, or as necessary for legitimate statutory purposes. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting our Grievance Redressal Officer at gennia@gmail.com. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers or third-party advertisers. Data is shared only under strict technical safeguards with:

  • Participating Sellers: Limited strictly to the 4-digit counter token and redemption amount necessary to approve the discount at checkout.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Security Providers: Trusted hosting, database, and encrypted messaging partners operating under confidentiality contracts.
  • Law Enforcement & Regulators: When legally compelled under statutory court orders or applicable Indian legislation.

6. Data Retention & Secure Disposal

  • User Account Data: Maintained while your account remains active; permanently deleted or de-identified within 30 days upon verified account closure.
  • Offer, Coupon, Redemption & Rewards Ledger: Retained for 90 days after the relevant offer or transaction cycle, after which records are securely archived or de-identified.
  • Technical Diagnostic Logs: Diagnostic error logs are scrubbed of sensitive PII and automatically purged after 15 to 90 days.

7. Data Principal Rights (DPDPA, 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you are entitled to:

  • Right of Access: Obtain a summary of personal data being processed and identities of data fiduciaries with whom it was shared;
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Request correction of inaccurate information or erasure of data no longer required;
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity;
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Submit a complaint regarding data processing directly to our Grievance Redressal Officer.
