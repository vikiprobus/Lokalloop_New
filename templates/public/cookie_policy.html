LOKALLOOP COOKIE POLICY

Effective Date: 24 October 2026 • Last Updated: 24 October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how LokalLoop (operated by Trisha Infotech Solution) uses cookies and similar web technologies across our website and mobile application.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device when you visit a website. They help maintain your signed-in state, protect against security vulnerabilities (such as Cross-Site Request Forgery), and ensure that essential platform features load correctly.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

A. Strictly Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are mandatory for the website to function securely and cannot be switched off in our systems:

  • Session Cookie (`sessionid`): Keeps you securely signed in to your buyer or merchant portal without needing to re-authenticate on every page.
  • CSRF Cookie (`csrftoken`): Cryptographic security token that protects your account from unauthorized Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks.
  • Consent State (`lokalloop_cookie_consent`): Remembers whether you have acknowledged our privacy banner.

B. Optional Performance & Analytics Cookies

With your permission, we may use aggregated analytics cookies to measure deal view engagement, discover which high-street corridors are popular, and troubleshoot platform speed. We do not use third-party cross-app advertising tracking or sell your browsing history to advertising networks.

3. Mobile App Device Identifiers

In mobile app environments, device tokens or local storage keys may be used instead of browser cookies to maintain your login session and verify counter redemption tokens. These are strictly used for security, offline caching, and functional troubleshooting.

4. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You can manage, inspect, or block cookies through your web browser settings at any time (e.g. Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Please note that if you disable strictly essential cookies, you will not be able to log in or access secure buyer or merchant portal features.

5. Contact Us

For questions regarding our use of cookies or privacy practices, please contact our Data Protection desk at gennia@gmail.com or review our full Privacy Policy.
