Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about in-store discounts, QR redemption, and merchant credits.

How do I claim a discount at the billing counter?

Simply present your smartphone at checkout and scan the merchant's physical acrylic QR standee using your camera or the in-app scanner. The green verified voucher screen will confirm your discount instantly to the cashier.

Do customers need to pay or buy coupons in advance?

No. LokalLoop never charges customers for coupons or vouchers. All discounts are free to browse and claim directly when paying your bill at the store counter.

How do promotional credits work for retail merchants?

Merchants only consume small promotional credits (₹5 per successful visit) when a paying customer actually redeems a discount. No upfront ad spend or monthly listing fees are charged.

Which cities are currently supported?

We are currently active across high-street offline retail corridors in Bengaluru (Indiranagar, Koramangala), Mumbai (Bandra West), and Pune (Koregaon Park).

Genuine Verified Reviews Coming Soon

In adherence to ethical commerce standards, we only publish 100% verified customer and merchant reviews following physical store visits. We never generate artificial or automated reviews.
