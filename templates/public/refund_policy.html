LOKALLOOP REFUND POLICY

Effective Date: 24 October 2026 • Last Updated: 24 October 2026

1. Platform Role & Commercial Model

LokalLoop (operated by Trisha Infotech Solution) is a technology platform that connects local buyers with neighborhood brick-and-mortar merchants and enables sellers to publish promotional counter discount offers.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, retail purchases, billing, payments for physical products, and order fulfilment occur directly between buyers and independent sellers at the store counter. GENNIA does not sell, ship, inventory, or supply any merchant merchandise.

2. Seller Promotional Credit Fees & Refunds

LokalLoop charges sellers promotional credits for publishing, hosting, and spotlighting discount campaigns across the network. The refund conditions applicable to merchant fees are:

  • Cancellation Before Publication: A merchant may cancel an unpublished offer draft before it goes live and receive a 100% full refund of the associated promotional credit fees.
  • Platform Technical Failure: If LokalLoop is technically unable to activate or publish an approved paid offer due to platform system errors, the merchant is entitled to a 100% full refund.
  • Post-Publication Policy (Non-Refundable): Once a promotional campaign is published and made live to the public on the network, all associated publishing fees and deducted promotional credits are strictly non-refundable. This applies regardless of whether the seller decides to pause the campaign early, whether coupons remain unredeemed, or whether the campaign ends before all coupons are exhausted.
  • Offer Expiry: An offer legally concludes when its maximum coupon allocation is exhausted or when its stated calendar duration expires. No refunds or credit rollovers are issued for unused coupons or remaining campaign time.
  • Refund Settlement: Approved refunds will be credited back to the original source payment method within 5 to 7 business days, subject to standard banking and payment gateway processing timelines.

3. In-Store Purchases & Retail Seller Responsibilities

Because physical merchandise is purchased directly at the merchant's physical billing counter:

  • The participating merchant is solely responsible for product quality, safety, condition, correct billing, honoring valid discount redemptions, and handling any retail cancellations, returns, replacements, or refunds as required under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
  • LokalLoop does not process buyer product payments and is not liable for merchant inventory defects or a seller's refusal to provide retail refunds for purchased items.
  • Buyers facing retail disputes should first request resolution directly from the merchant counter. If a merchant unfairly denies a published discount, buyers may file a report with GENNIA support for mediation and seller audit.

4. Buyer Points & Loyalty Rewards

  • LokalLoop awards promotional reward points to shoppers for using the platform. Buyers do not pay any monetary fee to earn these points.
  • Points, coupons, and redeemed vouchers are strictly promotional instruments and carry zero monetary value. They cannot be exchanged, transferred, or redeemed for cash, cheques, bank transfers, or refunds.
  • Expired rewards cannot be restored or reissued.
  • If a participating outlet is temporarily unable to redeem a reward voucher, LokalLoop support may investigate the incident, but does not guarantee cash compensation.
